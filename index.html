BEHIND THE NUMBERS

DATA METHODOLOGY

Clear sourcing and transparent limits for every published number.

01

Match results

Final scores are taken from the MLS NEXT Academy Division schedule snapshot. Standings are independently calculated from completed matches and are not an official league-published table.

02

Standings formula

Win = 3 points; draw = 1 point; loss = 0. PPM = points divided by matches played. Equal PPM is ordered by goal difference and goals scored for display, not as a claim about official tiebreak rules.

03

Golden Boot

Player totals count available original source-attributed Footy Access scoring events only. Inferred club assignments, unresolved events and own goals are excluded. This leaderboard is provisional.

04

Audit limitations

Seven events have unresolved club attribution, two scorer events were not captured, four own-goal events are excluded, and 13 score-constrained assignments are not treated as source-confirmed player-club goals. Audit closed with documented limitations.
