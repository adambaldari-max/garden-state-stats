BEHIND THE NUMBERS

DATA METHODOLOGY

Clear sourcing and transparent limits for every published number.

01

Match results

Final scores are taken from the MLS NEXT Academy Division schedule snapshot. Standings are independently calculated from completed matches and are not an official league-published table.

02

Standings formula

Win = 3 points; draw = 1 point; loss = 0. PPM = points divided by matches played. Equal PPM is ordered by goal difference and goals scored for display, not as a claim about official tiebreak rules.

03

Golden Boot

U13 lists provisional match-event scorer totals. U14 lists FootyAccess age-filtered MLS NEXT-wide goals for players at Garden State clubs; these totals may include non-conference matches. Neither is an official conference Golden Boot.

04

Audit limitations

U13 retains unresolved scorer attribution and missing events. U14 player totals are not yet reconciled to individual Garden State matches. Official match results and team standings are separate from provisional scorer data.
