DATA METHODOLOGY

Clear sourcing and transparent limits for every published number.

01

Match results

Final scores are taken from the MLS NEXT Academy Division schedule snapshot. Standings are independently calculated from completed matches and are not an official league-published table.

01Match results

Final scores are taken from the MLS NEXT Academy Division schedule snapshot. Standings are independently calculated from completed matches and are not an official league-published table.

02

Standings formula

Win = 3 points; draw = 1 point; loss = 0. PPM = points divided by matches played. Equal PPM is ordered by goal difference and goals scored for display, not as a claim about official tiebreak rules.

02Standings formula

03

Golden Boot
