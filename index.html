Quality of Play (QoP) is not the same as the conference standings on this website. QoP is an official MLS NEXT performance ranking. Our standings are an independent points-per-match table; leading that table does not automatically confirm qualification.

Regional participation is distinct from qualifying for MLS NEXT Cup. No team on this site is shown as qualified unless confirmed by MLS NEXT.

How Quality of Play (QoP) works

For U13 and U14, MLS NEXT and TAKA assess the quality of significant match actions rather than relying only on wins, draws, losses and goals. Analysts evaluate each action's location, description and execution. TAKA calculates attacking, defensive and overall team scores, adjusts them for opponent quality, and aggregates them across the season.

What it means for qualification: the 20 Academy Division league-play places per age group are determined through official QoP rankings, not our independent points-per-match standings. A team leading our table is not necessarily leading QoP.

Availability: MLS NEXT says U13/U14 QoP standings are published on its platform, but we have not verified a publicly accessible 2026–27 Academy Division Garden State U13/U14 ranking feed. We will not display inferred scores or qualification positions.

02

Standings formula

Win = 3 points, draw = 1 point, loss = 0. PPM = points divided by recorded completed matches. This independent PPM table uses goal difference and goals scored for display only. MLS NEXT uses Quality of Play (QoP) standings for U13/U14 MLS NEXT Cup qualification, which cannot be calculated from scores alone. The Academy Division rulebook’s official league-standings provision covers U15–U19.

Golden Boot coverage
